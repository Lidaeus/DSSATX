## Figure 1：Morris 结果图

### English caption

**Figure 1. Morris screening of parameter effects across response families.** (a) Heatmaps show the mean Morris effect score of the focal parameters for each response family. The left panel covers general genetic responses and the right panel covers water–nitrogen responses. `W resp.`, `N resp.`, and `W×N` denote water response, nitrogen response, and water×nitrogen interaction, respectively. (b) Cross-season mean effect magnitude for the focal parameters; values summarize the three seasons and are conditional on the sampled parameter ranges and scenarios. Parameter codes are TRIFL, main-stem leaf appearance; XFRT, fruit partitioning; LFMAX, maximum leaf photosynthesis; EM-FL, emergence to flowering; RTNO3, root nitrate uptake; RTNH4, root ammonium uptake; XFINT, fruit partitioning interval; and NMOBMX, maximum nitrogen mobilization. The heatmap values are process-family summaries and should not be interpreted as pairwise interaction estimates.

### 中文图注

**图1. 不同响应过程族中的参数 Morris 敏感性筛选结果。** （a）热图展示核心参数在各响应过程族中的平均 Morris 效应得分；左图为通用遗传响应，右图为水氮响应。（b）核心参数的跨季平均效应量；数值为三季均值，重要性条件化于所采样的参数范围和情景。参数代码含义为：TRIFL，主茎叶片出现；XFRT，果实分配；LFMAX，最大叶片光合；EM-FL，出苗至开花；RTNO3，根系硝态氮吸收；RTNH4，根系铵态氮吸收；XFINT，果实分配间隔；NMOBMX，最大氮再动员量。热图中的过程族得分是过程组汇总指标，不应解释为特定参数对的交互效应估计。

### English results paragraph

Across the three seasons, the Morris screening separated the dominant controls of general genetic responses from those of water–nitrogen responses (Fig. 1). Within the genetic domain, TRIFL had the largest cross-season mean effect score (0.167), followed by XFRT (0.128), whereas LFMAX and EM-FL were smaller and similar in magnitude (0.056 and 0.058, respectively). The response-family heatmap showed that TRIFL was most influential for canopy, biomass, and harvest responses (0.269, 0.211, and 0.187), while EM-FL was concentrated in phenology (0.216). XFRT contributed across canopy, biomass, and harvest responses, with scores of 0.154, 0.193, and 0.163.

In the water–nitrogen domain, RTNO3 was clearly dominant, with a cross-season mean score of 0.334. Its largest family-level effects were associated with water×nitrogen interaction, nitrogen response, and water response (0.400, 0.330, and 0.273). RTNH4 was a secondary contributor (0.093), with stronger effects on water response and water×nitrogen interaction than on the nitrogen-response family (0.124, 0.116, and 0.038). XFINT and NMOBMX showed comparatively smaller cross-season effects (0.049 and 0.048). These results identify RTNO3, TRIFL, and XFRT as priority parameters for subsequent response-oriented calibration and profile analysis, while the scores remain conditional on the sampled parameter ranges and scenarios.

### 中文结果段落

三季 Morris 筛选结果显示，通用遗传响应与水氮响应具有不同的主导参数（图1）。在遗传参数域中，TRIFL 的跨季平均效应得分最高（0.167），其次为 XFRT（0.128）；LFMAX 和 EM-FL 的效应较小且接近（分别为 0.056 和 0.058）。过程族热图进一步表明，TRIFL 主要影响冠层、生物量和采收响应（分别为 0.269、0.211 和 0.187），而 EM-FL 的效应主要集中于物候响应（0.216）。XFRT 在冠层、生物量和采收响应中均表现出较明显作用，过程组得分分别为 0.154、0.193 和 0.163。

在水氮响应参数域中，RTNO3 的跨季平均效应得分最高（0.334）。其效应主要体现在水×氮交互、氮响应和水分响应过程族中，得分分别为 0.400、0.330 和 0.273。RTNH4 为次级贡献参数（0.093），在水分响应和水×氮交互中的效应高于氮响应（分别为 0.124、0.116 和 0.038）。XFINT 和 NMOBMX 的跨季平均效应相对较小（分别为 0.049 和 0.048）。因此，RTNO3、TRIFL 和 XFRT 可作为后续水氮响应率定和参数剖面分析的优先参数；但这些重要性结论仍取决于当前采样参数范围和情景设定。

## Figure S2：Sobol 结果图

### English caption

**Figure S2. Sobol first-order and total-effect decomposition for scalar scientific responses.** Results are shown for the convergence-validated XXQL2502 effect season at the current Sobol sample size (N = 256). Each facet displays the three parameters with the largest total-order effect (`ST`); open and filled points represent first-order (`S1`) and total-order (`ST`) effects, respectively, with paired-bootstrap uncertainty intervals. Purple segments indicate a supported aggregate interaction remainder (`ST−S1`); this quantity summarizes interaction involvement and is not a pairwise interaction estimate. Response-specific standard deviations and units are given in the facet headers. `N` denotes nitrogen and `W×N` denotes the water×nitrogen response. Estimates slightly outside [0, 1] are retained as finite-sample estimator outputs and should be interpreted together with their uncertainty intervals, not as literal variance fractions above 100%. The decomposition is conditional on the sampled parameter ranges and is not a pooled cross-season Sobol estimate.

### 中文图注

**图S2. 标量科学响应的一阶 Sobol 效应与总效应分解。** 结果来自通过数值收敛验证的 XXQL2502 效应季节，当前 Sobol 样本量为 N = 256。每个面板展示总效应（ST）最大的前三个参数；空心点和实心点分别表示一阶效应（S1）和总效应（ST），线段表示配对 bootstrap 不确定性区间。紫色线段表示得到支持的聚合交互余量（ST−S1）；该量表示交互参与的总体余量，不是特定参数对的交互效应估计。面板标题给出各响应的标准差和单位。N 表示氮素，W×N 表示水×氮响应。略超出 [0, 1] 的点估计保留为有限样本估计结果，应结合不确定性区间解释，不应理解为超过 100% 的实际方差份额。本图分解条件化于当前采样参数范围，不代表跨季合并的 Sobol 分解。

### English results paragraph

The Sobol decomposition showed strong response dependence in both parameter importance and the relative contribution of interactions (Fig. S2). For anthesis, EM-FL was the leading parameter (S1 = 0.509; ST = 0.597), followed by TO11 (S1 = 0.258; ST = 0.351). For canopy LAI AUC, TRIFL dominated (S1 = 1.095; ST = 0.885), whereas XFRT and FL-SD had much smaller total effects (0.067 and 0.029). The point estimates slightly above one are finite-sample estimator outputs and are interpreted as near-complete main-effect dominance within their uncertainty intervals. A similar TRIFL dominance was observed for aboveground biomass and season-end cumulative harvested dry mass, with ST values of 0.703 and 0.988, respectively.

The water–nitrogen response decomposition was more differentiated. TRIFL dominated the nitrogen-response fruit-mass scalar (S1 = 1.041; ST = 0.931), while RTNO3 and TO11 had small total effects (0.059 and 0.032). For the water-response scalar, TRIFL again had the largest total effect (ST = 0.899), whereas FL-SD and TB2 showed smaller but supported aggregate interaction remainders (ST−S1 = 0.145 and 0.118). For the explicit W×N interaction response, TRIFL had the largest total effect (ST = 0.808), followed by TB2 (0.200) and FL-SD (0.175). Together, these results indicate that the simulated water–nitrogen response is controlled primarily by the canopy-development parameter TRIFL, with additional response-specific contributions from root nitrate uptake and parameters associated with interaction involvement. Because the current evidence is restricted to one convergence-validated season, these results should be treated as response-conditional rather than universal parameter rankings.

### 中文结果段落

Sobol 分解表明，参数重要性及交互参与程度均具有明显的响应依赖性（图S2）。在开花期响应中，EM-FL 为首要参数（S1 = 0.509，ST = 0.597），其次为 TO11（S1 = 0.258，ST = 0.351）。在冠层 LAI AUC 响应中，TRIFL 占主导地位（S1 = 1.095，ST = 0.885），XFRT 和 FL-SD 的总效应相对较小（分别为 0.067 和 0.029）。略高于 1 的点估计属于有限样本估计结果，应结合不确定性区间理解为接近完全的主效应占比，而不是字面意义上超过 100% 的方差份额。在地上部生物量和季末累计收获果实干重响应中，也观察到 TRIFL 的主导作用，其 ST 分别为 0.703 和 0.988。

水氮响应的参数贡献则表现出更强的响应特异性。在氮响应果实干重标量中，TRIFL 的作用最大（S1 = 1.041，ST = 0.931），RTNO3 和 TO11 的总效应较小（分别为 0.059 和 0.032）。在水分响应标量中，TRIFL 仍具有最大的总效应（ST = 0.899）；FL-SD 和 TB2 的总效应较小，但其聚合交互余量得到支持（ST−S1 分别为 0.145 和 0.118）。对于显式水×氮交互响应，TRIFL 的总效应最大（ST = 0.808），其次为 TB2（0.200）和 FL-SD（0.175）。总体而言，模拟水氮响应主要受冠层发育参数 TRIFL 控制，同时根系硝态氮吸收参数及部分交互相关参数对特定响应具有补充作用。由于当前证据仅来自一个通过收敛验证的季节，上述结果应解释为响应条件化的重要性，而不应视为跨季通用的参数排名。
